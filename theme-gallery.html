SNOWSHIFT / WORLD PROGRESSION

One vector racing system.
Four distinct worlds.

Four visual identities for one mathematically consistent game. The same track shape is rendered in every panel, to compare colours, shoreline visibility and scenery fairly.

Playable now: Beginner and Intermediate. Visual concepts: Advanced and Expert — those tracks are planned, not yet available.
